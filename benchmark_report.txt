CORA runnable benchmark extension
Date: 11 October 2026, Europe/Paris

Purpose and scope
The user authorized extending the minimal PoC to run baselines, save results,
and produce benchmark screenshots and animated GIFs. The implementation adds
configurable experiment execution, resume support, oracle-isolated evaluation,
upstream learner adapters, frozen trained-policy intervention evaluation,
statistics, plots and a local HTML results gallery. The default execution is
technical validation; no publication claim or convergence finding is inferred.
Original manuscript/template files and the original PoC are preserved.

Runnable intervention methods
cora: public dependency-family ridge model, decision-focused acquisition from
  draft Eq. 12, pessimistic diagnostic commit with no-op and identifiability checks.
random: uniform admissible experiments, randomly chosen admissible final edit.
uniform_tabular: one-hot arm-value ridge estimator, uniform experiments, greedy commit.
global_linear: intercept plus elementary indicators, uniform experiments, greedy commit.
global_neural: 32/32 tanh global MLP trained on observed interventions only, uniform
  experiments, greedy commit; forty tiny estimator optimization steps per budget.
factorized_uniform: same monomial family/ridge as CORA, uniform acquisition,
  greedy commit. Compare against cora_no_confidence to isolate active design.
structure_blind_active: one-hot arm model, same decision-difference acquisition,
  greedy commit. Compare against cora_no_confidence to isolate the representation.
cora_no_confidence: CORA features/acquisition with greedy commit. Compare directly
  with cora to isolate conservative commitment.
cora_incomplete_graph: CORA with singleton-only interaction structure.
cora_no_constraints: synthetic-only ablation allowing forbidden edit combinations;
  invalid acquisitions are logged and invalid decisions are counted. Simple Spread
  transactions remain validated, and this unsupported combination is reported.
greedy_swap: internal one-edit-at-a-time experiment/selection heuristic.
no_op: zero experiments, retain initial organization.
oracle: zero learning experiments, privileged maximum admissible reference net value.
  Oracle computation is separately counted and never given to learned methods.

They have not been invented or silently replaced with internal heuristics.
The user was asked for the two references while independent work continued.

Benchmarks and estimands
Synthetic: generated finite set functions with known coefficients and constrained
  edit combinations. Controlled edits, interaction order/density, observation
  noise, edit costs, forbidden pairs, sparse/dense public dependencies, deliberately
  missing dependencies, and reference-to-deployment policy shift are configurable.
  The exact enumerated runner supports 2–10 edits; it deliberately does not claim
  combinatorial scalability. Coefficients/true values belong to SyntheticTrial,
  not PublicProblem. Methods receive only public binary candidates, costs,
  admissibility, declared feature family, and logged observed scalar outcomes.
Simple Spread: unchanged JaxMARL dynamics/rewards, organization-conditioned
  observations, ordered atomic swaps, and frozen guided/random/trained actors.
  Policies are trained separately, then held fixed throughout every intervention
  dataset. Synthetic experiments exercise policy shift; this suite does not
  implement online adaptive-policy causal estimation or distributed commit.
  The conditional estimand fixes one reset state/context per seed and a finite
  horizon. Experiment rollout streams are matched by experiment index across
  methods. Oracle Monte Carlo streams and final replay streams are separate.
  Final replays for all methods within a policy condition use the same initial
  physical state, environment noise stream and policy PRNG stream.

Existing MARL implementations
Reuse unchanged pinned JaxMARL commit 976aeb152cb184a5095021968bba94da96eb6394:
  IPPO: baselines/IPPO/ippo_ff_mpe.py (feed-forward actor/local value).
  MAPPO: baselines/MAPPO/mappo_rnn_mpe.py (recurrent local actor/centralized critic).
  IQL/VDN/QMIX: baselines/QLearning/{iql_rnn,vdn_rnn,qmix_rnn}.py.
Each trainer source is checked against Git HEAD and its SHA256 is saved.
Environment/logging references are injected into a privately loaded module.
No remote WandB session is initialized or metrics transmitted. Q-learning's
public agent-identity observation suffix is reproduced at actor evaluation.
Checkpoint serialization preserves the actor and, separately, the complete
train states including MAPPO critic and Q-learning mixer/target state.
IPPO and MAPPO sample their learned policies; Q-learning uses greedy evaluation,
following the upstream evaluation convention. Different policy families are
distinct continuation protocols, not identical estimands.

Budget and reproducibility protocol
Every learned intervention method uses exactly the allocated number of oracle
queries, including a common initial no-op experiment. No-op and oracle references
have zero learning-query budgets, explicitly distinguished from other methods.
Uniform selection uses replacement after the initial no-op; adaptive tie-breaking
logs its actual conditional propensity. A deterministic learned design has no
general positivity guarantee. Contexts, policy versions, seed configuration,
interventions, outcomes, costs, propensities and model predictions are saved.
MARL total environment budgets include Q-learning's replay-shape initialization
trajectory; configuration division must be exact. The technical configuration
allocates twenty environment interactions per learner/seed: PPO collects twenty
training steps, whereas Q learning collects ten initialization and ten training
steps. Each uses one optimizer update; MAPPO updates actor and critic together.
The twenty steps are training interactions only. Evaluation/oracle interactions
are extra, with oracle replication counts explicitly stored. Different network
architectures and optimizer choices follow upstream defaults; there is no tuning.
Resume skips completed method files and reuses saved learner checkpoints only
when the same configuration fingerprint is supplied. A changed configuration
requires a new output directory. Intermediate per-method files are atomic writes;
failure tracebacks are retained. Larger budgets are possible without altering
the PoC runtime guards. The research configuration is an unexecuted example.

Metrics and statistical outputs
Budget-indexed held-out value MAE and effect-difference MAE exclude queried
interventions. If every valid candidate was queried, held-out error is null,
not an artificial zero. Simple regret, chosen net value, no-op rate, selected
constraint violations, query costs, compute time, model rank, diagnostic coverage,
and first recorded budget reaching a configured regret threshold are saved.
Invalid ablation decisions have null admissible regret and explicit violations.
Synthetic truth is exact. MPE values are Monte Carlo references, not exact causal
truth. Their standard errors and raw replicate values are saved; harmful net-value
labels use that reference. Paired reference standard errors and a diagnostic sign
resolution indicator distinguish uncertain harm classifications. Oracle maximization
over noisy estimates can be optimistic, especially with four technical replicates.
Rank/row-space checks are algebraic model identifiability, not causal identification.
Conservative radii remain configurable leverage heuristics. Structural/shift bounds
are not certified; every learned decision reports certified=false.
Across seeds, save mean/median/IQM and seed-bootstrap descriptive intervals.
IQM uses fractional quartile boundaries. Paired regret tests are reported only
with at least three paired seeds and receive Holm correction over all reported
tests. Two-seed technical intervals cannot support research conclusions. Merely
meeting a ten-seed recommendation does not establish power or sound assumptions.

Visual outputs
All plots derive from saved raw outcomes. Produce regret/error/harm/constraint/
abstention/compute curves, per-seed regret heatmaps, predicted-versus-reference
panels, true-versus-estimated synthetic pairwise Mobius interaction heatmaps,
and algorithm-specific training diagnostics. Training losses are not displayed
as directly comparable performance scores. Pairwise plots omit higher orders
and identify oracle-only forbidden combinations.
Save native MPE state arrays, actions, rewards and assignments. Reconstruct the
existing MPEVisualizer for faithful individual frames and Pillow-encoded GIFs.
Save first/middle/last PNG screenshots and explicit organization diagrams.
Generate synchronized comparison PNGs/GIFs using common plot bounds and matched
initial states; compare no-op, CORA, factorized-uniform and structure-blind-active.
The configured visualization seed is fixed before execution, rather than picked
for favorable outcomes. Local index.html links figures, CSV/JSON, screenshots,
raw traces, individual animations and comparisons. No hosting or publication.

Compatibility and remaining scientific scope
Recurrent adapters exposed Flax 0.10.4 calling a missing JAX scan API; Flax 0.10.2
works with JAX 0.4.38. Flashbax 0.1.3 supplies the existing replay implementation.
The updated principal and transitive dependencies are pinned. No new MARL
algorithm, optimizer, simulator, or rendering engine was invented.
This does not complete four-domain CORA-Bench, causal graph discovery, a temporal
SCM, calibrated confidence bounds, policy-shift certification, continuous online
adaptation, decentralized organizational consensus, or the two unnamed external
reproductions. Those require additional authoritative scientific definitions.
